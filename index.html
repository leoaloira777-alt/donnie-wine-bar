The Place

Donnie is a place for wine, music and good nights.

Tucked into Willemoesgade, Donnie is a contemporary wine bar built around long tables, low light and a soundtrack that carries the room from early evening into the small hours. It's a place people return to — for a glass before dinner, or for the night itself.
